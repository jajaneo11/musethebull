Methodology

How the Bullshit Meter works

every scan runs four layers. each layer adds evidence — and points — to the 0–100 score. warnings fire at 70+.

Layer 01

Contract scan

honeypot simulation (buy + sell), buy/sell taxes, mint & freeze authority states, LP size and lock/burn status, holder concentration. the on-chain facts.

Layer 02

Deployer intel

who launched this? full deployer history: how many tokens they've shipped, how many died, any rugged history. serial ruggers are repeat offenders — this layer catches them.

Layer 03

Bundle x-ray

traces top holders' funding sources to detect coordinated wallet clusters — one actor pretending to be fifty buyers. diverse funders = real distribution.

Layer 04

Sniper radar

first-block buyer analysis: fresh-wallet snipers, bot patterns, insider accumulation in the opening blocks. the launch tells you everything.

flags, not verdicts. a score is a starting point, not a court ruling. simulators can be fooled and contracts can change — always DYOR. what we promise: every flag comes with its evidence, and every miss gets logged here.
