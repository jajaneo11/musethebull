The Big Idea

MUSETHEBULL today is a funny roast account. Project RECEIPTS makes it a roast account that is provably right and genuinely useful. Two engines: Take Receipts records every falsifiable claim as a timestamped receipt and settles it later — building a public accuracy scoreboard. Rug Radar scans on-chain data for red flags and posts warnings in the bull's voice. Roast = entertainment. Receipts = credibility. Radar = utility. All three spin one flywheel.

Take Receipts

Every roast that contains a falsifiable claim becomes a receipt. Receipts can't be deleted — only settled.

How it works

  • When a roast contains a falsifiable claim ("X will rug within 30 days", "token Y is a honeypot"), the agent writes one receipt to receipts.json: id, claim, target, deadline, tweet_url, timestamp.
  • Tiered deadlines. Radar receipts: 24–72 hours (WON if price drops >90%, liquidity is pulled, or a honeypot activates). Slow-burn predictions ("project X will fade — it has no product"): 7–90 days.
  • Settlement is a single API check at deadline (price / liquidity vs. issuance) — not continuous monitoring. Results: WON / LOST / VOID (unverifiable claims are voided, never counted as losses).
  • LOST receipts are posted openly too. Transparency is the brand — a bull that admits mistakes is more trusted, not less.

Anti-gaming rules

  • Only the agent issues receipts. No user-minted receipts.
  • Claims must be specific and falsifiable, with a clear deadline.
  • VOID results don't damage the score.

The viral loop

  • People tag @musethebull to "receipt" influencer predictions.
  • Influencers whose predictions fail enter a permanent Hall of Shame on this site.
  • Followers stop being spectators and become participants.

Rug Radar

An automated scanner that reads on-chain data for red flags — and warns in the bull's voice when something stinks.

Chain scope

  • Solana + Base at launch. Solana is the biggest memecoin casino (highest rug volume); Base is the fastest-growing scene with strong CT mindshare. Both are fully supported by the DexScreener and GoPlus APIs.
  • BSC is optional in Phase 2. Ethereum mainnet is not a priority for early rugs — gas is too expensive for micro-rugs there.

How it works

  • A cron job scans trending / newly listed pairs every 1–2 hours via the DexScreener API.
  • Each candidate is checked against the GoPlus Security API: honeypot? buy/sell tax? ownership renounced? LP locked or burned? dev wallet concentration?
  • The findings feed the Bullshit Meter — a 0–100 score.
  • Score ≥ 70 triggers a warning roast with the contract and the top red flags. Max 2 radar posts per day.
  • Wording is always "flags, not verdicts" — in character: "not financial advice, I'm just a bull that reads contracts."

False-positive plan

  • Every warning is labeled as a flag, never a verdict.
  • If a project proves clean, the agent posts an open correction.

The Flywheel

How the two engines combine into one loop.

1

Radar flags a suspicious token.

2

Roast — the warning goes out in the bull's voice.

3

Receipt — auto-issued: "X will rug/dump within 72h."

4

Settlement — one API check at deadline. WON receipts prove the radar works.

5

Credibility — the scoreboard grows, followers grow, targets multiply. Back to step 1.

Risks

Known risks, stated plainly, with mitigations.

  • False positives → labeled as flags; open corrections.
  • "Financial advice" accusations → in-character disclaimers on every warning; opinions, not instructions.
  • API limits → caching, spaced crons; free tiers suffice for v1.
  • X spam perception → 2 radar posts/day cap; everything else lives on this site.
  • LOST receipts → posted openly as content, not hidden.
